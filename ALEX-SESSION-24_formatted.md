## Sending Regards — سلام برسون

### سلام برسون!

> **Salām beresoon!** — Send my regards!

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|
| سلام | salām | hello, regards |
| برسون | beresoon | deliver / send (imperative) |

**برسون** (beresoon) is the imperative form of the verb **رسوندن** (resoondan — to deliver / to send).

---

### Example Sentences

**به مریم سلام برسون!**
*Be Maryam salām beresoon!*
Say hi to Maryam! / Send my regards to Maryam!

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----:|
| به | be | to | preposition |
| مریم | Maryam | Maryam (name) | indirect object |
| سلام | salām | regards | direct object |
| برسون | beresoon | send/deliver | imperative verb |

---

**لطفاً به باباتـ، پدرت سلام برسون!**
*Lotfan be bābāt, pedaret salām beresoon!*
Please send my regards to your dad!

**لطفاً به مامانت، مادرت سلام برسون!**
*Lotfan be māmānet, mādaret salām beresoon!*
Please send my regards to your mom!

**لطفاً به برادرت سلام برسون!**
*Lotfan be barādaret salām beresoon!*
Please send my regards to your brother!

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|
| بابات | bābāt | your dad (informal) |
| پدرت | pedaret | your father (formal) |
| مامانت | māmānet | your mom (informal) |
| مادرت | mādaret | your mother (formal) |
| برادرت | barādaret | your brother |

---

**مامانم سلام میرسونه**
*Māmānam salām miresooneh*
My mom is sending regards.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----:|
| مامانم | māmānam | my mom | subject |
| سلام | salām | regards | direct object |
| میرسونه | miresooneh | is sending/delivering | present tense, 3rd person |

## Grammar: Imperative Sentences in Farsi

Imperative sentences express requests, commands, orders, and instructions. Like English, the subject "you" is dropped. However, Persian has **two** forms of "you":

| English | Farsi | Pinglish | Usage |
|:-------:|:-----:|:--------:|:-----:|
| You (singular) | تو | to | informal, peers, younger people |
| You (plural/formal) | شما | shomā | formal, older people, showing respect |

---

### Formation

**Singular Imperative (تو / to):**
> Add the prefix **بِ** (be-) to the present stem. No verb ending.

**Plural/Formal Imperative (شما / shomā):**
> Add the prefix **بِ** (be-) to the present stem + add the ending **ـید** (-id).

| Infinitive | Present Stem | Singular (تو) | Plural/Formal (شما) |
|:----------:|:------------:|:--------------:|:--------------------:|
| خندیدن (khandidan) — to laugh | خند (khand) | **بخند** (bekhand) | **بخندید** (bekhandid) |

---

### Negative Imperative

To form the negative, replace prefix **بِ** (be-) with **نَ** (na-):

| Positive | Negative |
|:--------:|:--------:|
| بخند (bekhand) — Laugh! | **نخند** (nakhand) — Don't laugh! |

---

### Imperative with Pronouns

**بگو** (begoo) — Tell!
- **به من بگو** — *be man begoo* — Tell me!
- **به اون بگو** — *be oon begoo* — Tell her/him!

**بده** (bedeh) — Give!
- **به من بده** — *be man bedeh* — Give me!

**برید** (berid) — Go! (formal/plural form of برو)

## خواندن (khāndan / khoondan) — To Read

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|
| خواندن | khāndan | to read (original/formal pronunciation) |
| خوندن | khoondan | to read (colloquial pronunciation) |

### Example Sentences

**من کتاب میخونم.**
*Man ketāb mikhoonam.*
I read books.

**من اصلاً کتاب نمیخونم.**
*Man aslan ketāb nemikhoonam.*
I don't read books at all.

## شلوغ — Sholugh — Busy / Crowded

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meanings |
|:-----:|:--------:|:--------:|
| شلوغ | sholugh | busy, active (people or places) / crowded, full of people (places) |

### For Places

**کافه شلوغ هست.**
*Cafe sholugh hast.*
The café is busy. / The café is crowded.

### For People — سرم شلوغه (Saram sholugh-e)

When talking about **people** being busy, Persian uses the construction **سَرَ... شلوغه** (sar-e ... sholugh-e), literally "one's head is busy":

**سرم شلوغ هست.**
*Saram sholugh hast.*
I'm busy.

**پدرم سرش شلوغه!**
*Pedaram saresh sholugh-e!*
My dad is busy!

**سر بابام این هفته شلوغه!**
*Sar-e bābām in hafte sholugh-e!*
My dad is busy this week!

**امروز سرت شلوغه؟**
*Emrooz saret sholugh-e?*
Are you busy today?

**شما این هفته سرتون شلوغه؟**
*Shomā in hafte saretoon sholugh-e?*
Are you (formal) busy this week?

**مایلا این ماه خیلی سرش شلوغه.**
*Mayla in māh kheili saresh sholugh-e.*
Mayla is very busy this month.

## خیلی مهم! — KHEILI MOHEM! — Very Important!

### Pronunciation Pairs: bad / bād / baad

These words look similar in Pinglish but have **different** vowel lengths and meanings:

| Pinglish | Farsi | Meaning | Vowel |
|:--------:|:-----:|:-------:|:-----:|
| bad | بَد | bad | short 'a' |
| bād | باد | wind | long 'ā' |
| bādi | بادی | windy | long 'ā' + i |
| ba'd az zohr | بعد از ظهر | afternoon | short 'a' with glottal |
| ba'd | بعد | then, after | short 'a' with glottal |
| bāzi | بازی | play, game | long 'ā' |

## Reading Comprehension — رستوران سنتی ما

### The Passage

**رستوران سنتی ما**

من عاشق خانواده‌ام هستم. ما در یزد زندگی میکنیم. یزد یک شهر در ایران هست. ما یک رستوران داریم. ما همیشه غذای سنتی در رستوران‌مان میپزیم. رستوران ما خیلی قشنگ و قدیمی هست. مامانم معمولاً آشپزی میکنه و مامان‌بزرگم ترشی درست میکنه. برادر بزرگم هم در رستوران کار میکنه و شیرینی و دسر درست میکنه. من در دانشگاه درس میخونم، ولی عصرها در رستوران‌مان کار میکنم. من هیچ‌وقت آشپزی نمیکنم ولی ظرف‌ها رو میشورم و بعضی وقت‌ها آشپزخونه و دستشویی رو تمیز میکنم. رستوران امروز خیلی شلوغه. ما چهار خورشت خوشمزه و چند دسر تازه آماده میکنیم. خورشت بادمجان غذای مورد علاقه‌ی منه.

---

### Translation

Our Traditional Restaurant

I love my family. We live in Yazd. Yazd is a city in Iran. We have a restaurant. We always cook traditional food in our restaurant. Our restaurant is very beautiful and old. My mom usually cooks, and my grandma makes pickles. My older brother also works in the restaurant and makes sweets and desserts. I study at university, but I work in our restaurant in the evenings. I never cook, but I wash the dishes and sometimes clean the kitchen and restroom. The restaurant is very busy today. We will prepare four delicious stews and a few fresh desserts. Aubergine stew is my favourite.
