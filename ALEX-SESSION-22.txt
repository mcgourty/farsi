GRAMMAR: Present tense of "to be" verb (budan)

Man hastam - I am
To hasti - You are
Oon hast/ast/eh - He/She is

Mā hastim - We are
Shomā hastid - You are (plural/formal)
Ānhā/Oonhā hastand - They are

---

SUBJECT PRONOUNS:

او - Oon - He, she
آنها - oonhā - They

DEMONSTRATIVE ADJECTIVES:

این - in - this
اینها - In hā - these
اون/آن - un - That
اونها/آنها - unhā - those
اینجا - Inja - Here
آنجا - unjā - there

---

ADJECTIVE + "to be" (colloquial):

Man khoobam - I am good
To khoobi - You are good
Oon khoob hast/ast/eh - He/She is good

Mā khoobim - We are good
Shomā khoobid - You are good (pl/formal)
Oonha khooband - They are good

---

ASKING "HOW" - Chetor, chetori

To chetor hasti? / To Chetori? - How are you?
Shoma chetor hastid? / Shoma chetorid? - How are you? (formal)
oon Chetor hast? / Oon chetor-eh? - How is he/she?
Oonha chetor hastand? / Oona chetorand? - How are they?
