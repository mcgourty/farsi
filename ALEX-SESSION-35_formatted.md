# Session 35 - Full Breakdown

> **پارسی را پاس بداریم** — *Pārsi rā pās bedārim* — Protect Persian Language

**Theme of this session:** *where things are.* Prepositions of place (توی، روی، زیرِ، کنارِ…), the kitchen, the verbs **برداشتن** (to pick up) and **گذاشتن** (to put), compass directions and geography, and the many jobs of **قرار**.

---

## Cultural Note: Iran

Iran is a large country in **Western Asia**. It covers about **1.65 million square kilometres**, making it the **second-largest country in the Middle East**. Its population is around **88 million**, one of the most populous in the region.

## Part 1 — Directions: up, down, right, left

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| بالا | bālā | up | ب-ا-ل-ا |
| پایین | pāyin | down | پ-ا-ی-ی-ن |

| چپ | chap | left | چ-پ |

|-------|----------|---------|------|

| بالا | bālā | up | noun ("ups") |

## Part 2 — تو / توی — In, Inside

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| غذا | ghazā | food | غ-ذ-ا |
| یخ | yakh | ice | ی-خ |
| یخچال | yakhchāl | fridge, refrigerator | یخ (ice) + چال (pit) |
| تو / توی | tu / tuye | in, inside | ت-و (+ ی before a noun) |

**Letter Breakdown — یخچال:**
- ی (ye) - /y/
- خ (khe) - /kh/
- چ (che) - /ch/
- ا (alef) - /ā/
- ل (lām) - /l/

→ Literally an "ice pit". **یخ** (ice) is a word in its own right.

**غذا کجا هست؟**
*Ghazā kojā hast?*
Where is the food?

**غذا توی یخچاله.**
*Ghazā tuye yakhchāl-eh.*
The food is in the fridge.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| غذا | ghazā | food | subject |
| توی | tuye | in | preposition + ezāfe |
| یخچال | yakhchāl | fridge | object of preposition |
| ـه | -eh | is | spoken است, attached |

**تخم مرغ‌ها توی یخچال نیستند.**
*Tokhm-e morgh hā tuye yakhchāl nistand.*
The eggs are not in the fridge.

**میوه‌ها توی یخچال بودند.**
*Mive hā tuye yakhchāl boodand.*
(But) the fruits were in the fridge.

**کیفش توی ماشین نبود.**
*Kifesh tuye māshin nabood.*
His bag was not in the car.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| کیف | kif | bag | subject |
| ـش | -esh | his / her | possessive suffix |
| توی ماشین | tuye māshin | in the car | location |
| نبود | nabood | was not | نـ + بود |

## Part 3 — Socks and the wardrobe

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| جوراب | joorāb | sock | ج-و-ر-ا-ب — plural جوراب‌ها |
| کمد | komod | wardrobe, chest of drawers | ک-م-د |

*Joorāb hāye man kojā hastand?*
Where are my socks?

## Part 4 — Keys and handles

|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| کلید | kelid / kilid | key | ک-ل-ی-د |

| دسته | dasteh | handle | د-س-ت-ه |
| دسته‌گل | dasteh gol | bouquet | دسته (bunch) + گل (flower) |

- ک (kāf) - /k/
- ل (lām) - /l/

|-------|----------|---------|------|
| تو | to | you | subject |
| می‌دونی | midooni | (you) know | verb, دونستن |

## Part 5 — رو / روی — On, On Top Of

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| رو / روی | ru / ruye | on, on top of | ر-و (+ ی before a noun) |
| میز | miz | table | م-ی-ز |
| لباس | lebās | clothes | ل-ب-ا-س |
| موبایل | mobāyl / mobil | mobile phone | from English |
| صندلی | sandali | chair | ص-ن-د-ل-ی |

**ببین! کلیدت روی میزه.**
*Bebin! Kelidet ruye miz-eh.*
Look! Your key is on the table.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| ببین | bebin | look! | imperative, دیدن |
| کلیدت | kelidet | your key | کلید + ت |
| روی | ruye | on | preposition + ezāfe |
| میز | miz | table | object of preposition |
| ـه | -eh | is | spoken است |

**لباس‌هات روی تخت هستند.**
*Lebās hāt ruye takht hastand.*
Your clothes are on the bed.

**لباس‌هام روی تخت‌اند.**
*Lebās hām ruye takht-and.*
My clothes are on the bed.
→ The teacher writes *(hast)and*: **هستند** can shrink to **ـند** after a word.

**لباس‌هام توی کمدند.**
*Lebās hām tuye komod-and.*
My clothes are in the wardrobe.

**موبایلم کجا بود؟**
*Mobāylam kojā bood?*
Where was my phone?

**اینجا، روی این صندلی.**
*Injā, ruye in sandali.*
Here, on this chair.

## Part 7 — زیر / زیرِ — Under, Beneath

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| زیر / زیرِ | zir / zir-e | under, beneath | ز-ی-ر |
| کفش | kafsh | shoe | ک-ف-ش |
| مبل | mobl | sofa, armchair | م-ب-ل |
| کاناپه | kānāpeh | sofa, couch | from French *canapé* |

**گربه زیر صندلی بود.**
*Gorbeh zir-e sandali bood.*
The cat was under the chair.

**کفش‌های تو زیر تخت نبودند.**
*Kafsh hāye to zir-e takht naboodand.*
Your shoes weren't under the bed.

**کفش‌هات زیر تخت نبودند.**
*Kafsh hāt zir-e takht naboodand.*
Your shoes weren't under the bed. — spoken

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| کفش‌ها | kafsh hā | shoes | subject, plural |
| ـت | -t | your | replaces *-ye to* |
| زیرِ | zir-e | under | preposition + ezāfe |
| تخت | takht | bed | object of preposition |
| نبودند | naboodand | were not | نـ + بودند |

*Oonhā zir-e mobl hastand!*

## Part 8 — کنار / کنارِ — Beside, Next To

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| کنار / کنارِ | kenār / kenār-e | beside, next to | ک-ن-ا-ر |
| زن | zan | wife; woman | ز-ن |

**سارا کنار من هست.**
*Sārā kenār-e man hast.*
Sara is next to me.

**علی کنار زنشه.**
*Ali kenār-e zanesh-eh.*
Ali is next to his wife.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| علی | Ali | Ali | subject |
| کنارِ | kenār-e | next to | preposition + ezāfe |
| زن | zan | wife | object of preposition |
| ـش | -esh | his | possessive suffix |
| ـه | -eh | is | spoken است |

**کنار دوستم** — *Kenār-e doostam* — next to my friend
**کنار تو** — *Kenār-e to* — next to you

**من کنار تو می‌شینم.**
*Man kenār-e to mishinam.*
I sit next to you.

**ما کنار هم می‌شینیم.**
*Mā kenār-e ham(dige) mishinim.*
We sit next to each other.
→ **هم** or **همدیگه** = each other.

## Part 9 — The Preposition Table

Every preposition of place has a **bare form** (used alone: *"it's under"*) and a form **with the ezāfe** (used before a noun: *"under the chair"*). For words ending in a vowel the ezāfe is written **ی** (*-ye*); otherwise it is the unwritten kasreh **ِ** (*-e*).

| Meaning | Bare | Pinglish | Before a noun | Pinglish | Example |
|:--------|:----:|:--------:|:-------------:|:--------:|:--------|
| in, inside | تو | tu | توی | tuye | توی یخچال — in the fridge |
| on, on top of | رو | ru | روی | ruye | روی میز — on the table |
| under, beneath | زیر | zir | زیرِ | zir-e | زیرِ صندلی — under the chair |
| beside, next to | کنار | kenār | کنارِ | kenār-e | کنارِ من — next to me |
| in front of | جلو | jelo | جلوی | jeloye | جلوی خونه* — in front of the house |
| behind | پشت | posht | پشتِ | posht-e | پشتِ در* — behind the door |
| between | بین | beyn | بینِ | beyn-e | بینِ ما* — between us |
| between, amid (formal) | میان | miān | میانِ | miān-e | میانِ دو کشور* — between two countries |
| up → above | بالا | bālā | بالای | bālāye | بالای میز* — above the table |
| down → below | پایین | pāyin | پایینِ | pāyin-e | پایینِ صفحه* — at the bottom of the page |

→ The prepositions are from page 15 of the lesson. Examples marked * are agent-supplied to show the pattern; the rest come from the teacher's sentences. Only the teacher's sentences are on flashcards.

→ **پشت** also means *back* (the body part, Session 32). **بالا** and **پایین** are the *up* and *down* from Part 1; add the ezāfe and they become *above* and *below*.

## Part 10 — برداشتن — To Pick Up, To Take, To Remove

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|
| برداشتن | bardāshtan | to pick up, to take, to remove |

**Letter Breakdown — برداشتن:**
- ب (be) - /b/
- ر (re) - /r/
- د (dāl) - /d/
- ا (alef) - /ā/
- ش (shin) - /sh/
- ت (te) - /t/
- ن (noon) - /n/

→ It is **بر** (*bar*, "up") + **داشتن** (*dāshtan*, to have/hold): to "hold up". The prefix **بر** always stays at the front, so **می** and **ن** go *after* it: برمی‌دارم, برنداشتم.

| present | برمی‌دارم | barmidāram | I pick up |
| command | بردار / بردارید | bardār / bardārid | pick it up! |
| negative command | برندار / برندارید | barnadār / barnadārid | don't pick it up! |

## Part 11 — گذاشتن — To Put, To Place

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning |

## Part 12 — Practice: answers to check with your teacher

Pages 12 and 14 of the lesson are English-only prompts with no answers given. **The Farsi below is agent-suggested, not from the teacher** — check it in class. These are not on flashcards.

| English prompt (teacher) | Suggested Farsi (agent) | Pinglish |
|:-------------------------|:------------------------|:---------|
| Pick up the phone quickly! | گوشی رو زود بردار! | Gooshi ro zood bardār! |
| Have a sweet! (Take a sweet!) | یه شیرینی بردار! | Ye shirini bardār! |
| Remove | بردار / بردارید | Bardār / bardārid |
| I picked up your shirt from the floor. | پیراهنت رو از روی زمین برداشتم. | Pirāhanet ro az ruye zamin bardāshtam. |
| Somebody has taken my sandwich out of the fridge. | یکی ساندویچم رو از توی یخچال برداشته. | Yeki sāndevicham ro az tuye yakhchāl bardāshteh. |
| I took my hand off the table. | دستم رو از روی میز برداشتم. | Dastam ro az ruye miz bardāshtam. |
| They removed the map from the wall. | نقشه رو از روی دیوار برداشتن. | Naghsheh ro az ruye divār bardāshtan. |
| We put the chairs around the table. | صندلی‌ها رو دورِ میز گذاشتیم. | Sandali hā ro dor-e miz gozāshtim. |
| Mom put the salad bowl on the table and said, 'Don't touch it.' | مامان کاسه‌ی سالاد رو روی میز گذاشت و گفت: «بهش دست نزن.» | Māmān kāse-ye sālād ro ruye miz gozāsht va goft: "Behesh dast nazan." |

Notes on the suggestions:
- **از روی / از توی** — "off / out of": از (from) + the preposition. You take something *from on* the table, *from inside* the fridge.
- **برداشته** (*has taken*) is the present perfect, which hasn't been taught yet. A simple past **برداشت** (*took*) works too.
- **دست زدن به** (to touch) is from Session 33; **بهش** = به + اون (to it).
- New words in the suggestions, not yet taught: پیراهن (*pirāhan*, shirt), زمین (*zamin*, floor, ground), ساندویچ (*sāndevich*), دیوار (*divār*, wall), دورِ (*dor-e*, around), کاسه (*kāseh*, bowl), سالاد (*sālād*).

## Part 16 — قرار — Appointment, Restless, Located

One root, three uses this session.

### 1. قرار — an appointment, a meeting, a date

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| قرار | gharār | appointment, meeting, date | ق-ر-ا-ر |
| قرارِ کاری | gharār-e kāri | work appointment, business meeting | قرار + کار + ی |

**من قرار دارم.**
*Man gharār dāram.*
I have an appointment.

**من قرار کاری دارم.**
*Man gharār-e kāri dāram.*
I have a work appointment. / I have a business meeting.

**من با علی قرار دارم.**
*Man bā Ali gharār dāram.*
I'm meeting Ali.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| من | man | I | subject |
| با علی | bā Ali | with Ali | با + person |
| قرار | gharār | appointment | object |
| دارم | dāram | I have | verb, داشتن |

**ما با هم قرار داریم.**
*Mā bā ham(dige) gharār dārim.*

### 2. بی‌قرار — restless, anxious

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| بی‌قرار | bigharār | restless (physical: unable to relax); anxious (mental: worry) | بی (without) + قرار (calm) |

**بچه خیلی بی‌قرار هست.**
*Bacheh kheili bigharār hast.*
The kid is very fidgety.

**مامانم خیلی بی‌قرار هست، من نگرانش هستم.**
*Māmānam kheili bigharār hast, man negarānesh hastam.*
My mom is really anxious, and I'm worried about her.

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Role |
|-------|----------|---------|------|
| مامانم | māmānam | my mom | subject |
| خیلی | kheili | very | adverb |
| بی‌قرار | bigharār | anxious | adjective |
| هست | hast | is | verb |
| من | man | I | subject (2nd clause) |
| نگرانش | negarānesh | worried about her | نگران + ش |
| هستم | hastam | I am | verb |

### 3. قرار داشتن — to be located, to be situated

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|
| قرار داشتن | gharār dāshtan | to be located, to be situated |

This is the **formal, written** register, so it is **قرار دارد** (*dārad*), not the spoken *dāreh*.

> **[place] + در + [location] + قرار دارد**

**یه میز در مرکز اتاق قرار دارد.**
*Ye miz dar markaz-e otāgh gharār dārad.*
There is a table in the centre of the room.

**ایران در خاورمیانه قرار دارد.**
*Irān dar khāvar-e miāneh gharār dārad.*
Iran is located in the Middle East.

**هتل نزدیک ساحل قرار دارد.**
*Hotel nazdik-e sāhel gharār dārad.*
The hotel is located near the beach.

**خلیج فارس در جنوب ایران قرار دارد.**
*Khalij-e Fārs dar jonoob-e Irān gharār dārad.*
The Persian Gulf is located in the south of Iran.

|-------|----------|---------|------|

| در | dar | in | preposition |
| جنوبِ | jonoob-e | the south of | noun + ezāfe |
| ایران | Irān | Iran | |
| قرار دارد | gharār dārad | is located | verb (formal) |

| Farsi | Pinglish | Meaning | Breakdown |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-------:|:----------|
| مرکز | markaz | center | م-ر-ک-ز |
| اتاق | otāgh | room | ا-ت-ا-ق |
| خاورمیانه | khāvar-e miāneh | the Middle East | خاور (east) + میانه (middle) |

## Grammar Summary

### 1. Prepositions of place take the ezāfe before a noun

```
تو → توی یخچال        رو → روی میز        زیر → زیرِ صندلی
کنار → کنارِ من        جلو → جلوی …         پشت → پشتِ …
بالا → بالای …         پایین → پایینِ …      بین / میان → بینِ … / میانِ …
```

### 2. Where is it? — کجاست / کجان

| Full | Spoken | Meaning |
|:----:|:------:|:-------:|
| کجا هست؟ | کجاست؟ | where is it? |
| کجا هستند؟ | کجان؟ | where are they? |

### 3. "My socks" without من

| Full | Spoken | Meaning |
|:----:|:------:|:-------:|
| جوراب‌های من | جوراب‌هام | my socks |
| کفش‌های تو | کفش‌هات | your shoes |
| لباس‌های تو | لباس‌هات | your clothes |

### 4. هستند shortens after a word

```
روی تخت هستند → روی تخت‌اند     توی کمد هستند → توی کمدند
```

### 5. بر- is a prefix that stays in front

```
برمی‌دارم   (bar + mi + dār + am)    I pick up
برنداشتم    (bar + na + dāsht + am)  I didn't pick up
بردار       (bar + dār)              pick it up!
```

### 6. گذار is spoken ذار

```
می‌گذارم → می‌ذارم     بگذار → بذار     نگذار → نذار
```

### 7. قرار in three jobs

| Farsi | Meaning |
|:-----:|:-------:|
| قرار داشتن (با) | to have an appointment (with) |
| بی‌قرار | restless, anxious |
| قرار دارد | is located (formal) |

### 8. Suffixes from this session

| Suffix | Job | Example |
|:------:|:----|:--------|
| ـی | makes an adjective | شرق → شرقی، کار → کاری |
| بی‌ـ | without, -less | قرار → بی‌قرار |
