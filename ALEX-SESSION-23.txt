ALEX SESSION 23

پارسی را پاس بداریم
Pārsi rā pās bedārim
Protect Persian Language

---

QUOTE:

راه دَر جَهَان یکی است وَ آن راهِ راستی است
Rāh dar jahān yeki ast va ān rāh-e rāsti ast.
In this world, there is but one true path, the path of righteousness.

زَرَتُشْت - zartosht - Zarathustra
Zarathushtra was an Iranian religious reformer and prophet, traditionally regarded as the founder of Zoroastrianism.

---

MORE QUOTES:

نور بَر تاریکی پیروز است
Light triumphs over darkness.

پایانِ شَبِ سیَاه ، سفید است.
The end of the dark night is light.

VERB: خوردن - khordan - To eat, to drink

POSITIVE CONJUGATION:

Man goosht mikhoram - I eat meat
To goosht mikhori? - Do you eat meat?
Oon goosht mikhoreh - He/She eats meat
Ma goosht mikhorim - We eat meat
Shoma goosht mikhorid? - Do you eat meat? (formal/plural)

NEGATIVE CONJUGATION:

Man māhi nemikhoram - I don't eat fish
To goosht nemikhori - You don't eat meat
Oon māhi nemikhoreh - He/She doesn't eat fish
Ma māhi nemikhorim - We don't eat fish
Shoma māhi nemikhorid - You don't eat fish (formal/plural)

---

DIALOGUE PRACTICE:

Har do - both
Man har do ro doost dāram - I like both

-Alan chāy mikhori? -Are alan chāy mikhoram.
-Do you want tea now? -Yes, I'm having tea now.

-Shekar mikhāy? -Na, kheili mamnoon.
-Do you want sugar? -No, thank you very much.

-Āb mikhori? -Are mikhoram, mamnoon.
-Do you want water? -Yes please, thank you.

-Bastani mikhori? -Na, man aslan bastani nemikhoram.
-Do you want ice cream? -No, I don't eat ice cream at all.
